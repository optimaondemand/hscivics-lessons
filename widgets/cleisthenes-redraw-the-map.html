Redraw the Map

A scaled-down model of Cleisthenes's reform. Each tribe below is a bundle of three "thirds" (trittyes). Tap one third, then tap another to swap them. Your goal: make it impossible for any one region's patrons to carry a whole tribe.

City thirds (C) Coast thirds (S) Inland thirds (I)

Model only: the real reform drew ten tribes from thirty thirds. In the old arrangement each tribe sat inside one region, so the local patrons who ruled that region also ruled the tribe.
