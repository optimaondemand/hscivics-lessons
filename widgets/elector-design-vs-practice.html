Hamilton's design, 1788

    Practice since the early nineteenth century

      What changed was how electors behave. Hamilton pictured independent deliberators; organized parties turned them into pledged party loyalists. This describes how the institution developed, which is a separate question from whether it should be kept, reformed, or replaced.
