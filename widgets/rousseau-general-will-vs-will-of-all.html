Counting Wishes, or Asking What Serves Everyone

Tap a citizen to change the question that citizen asks before voting. Watch how the same village reaches different results.

The village
A river village of nine households has a spare fund and must spend it on one project. Four households fish, three farm, two keep market stalls. A hard famine year comes every so often, and the village has no shared storehouse.
Notice what the screen cannot show: what a citizen is really asking inside. Someone has to decide which ballots count as asking about the common good. That is the difficulty the founders saw in the theory.
